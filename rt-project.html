AI-Integrated RT film thickness measurement

(Internship Project)

Detail

This project focuses on the application of artificial intelligence techniques to integrate real-time (RT) film thickness measurement in materials science. The goal is to develop a system that can accurately measure and analyze film thickness in real-time, improving the efficiency and precision of the measurement process.

Our team developed an AI-based system that segments the pipe wall and step wedge, converts pixels into millimetres, measures thickness continuously along the pipe wall, and generates a heatmap showing thinner areas in red and thicker areas in green. The system can also automatically identify the minimum thickness in each pipe-wall section. As we increased the training dataset from around 30-40 images to 50-60 images, the model's results improved, showing strong potential for further development.
