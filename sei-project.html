SEI simulation using AIPFM

(Literature Review)

Detail

This review focuses on the application of the Allen-Cahn phase-field model (AIPFM) to simulate the formation and growth of the solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) and dendritic structures at the electrode–electrolyte interface. The AIPFM is a mathematical framework that describes the evolution of phase boundaries in materials, making it suitable for modeling complex electrochemical processes.

This review uses MATLAB as a tool to implement and visualize the phase-field models discussed in the literature. The MATLAB code will be used to simulate the formation and growth of the SEI and dendritic structures, allowing for a better understanding of the underlying mechanisms and the effects of different parameters on the simulation process.

Source: Simulating solid electrolyte interphase formation spanning 108 time scales with an atomically informed phase-field mode article.
