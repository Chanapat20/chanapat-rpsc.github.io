My Projects

Simulations of SEI dendrite formation.

This simulation investigates the formation and growth of the solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) and dendritic structures at the electrode–electrolyte interface. By modelling electrochemical reactions, ion transport, and phase evolution, the simulation visualizes how variations in reaction rates and local material properties can cause non-uniform growth.

Simulations of Phase-field modeling of corrosion kinetics under dual-oxidants

This simulation investigates the formation and evolution of metal oxides when a material is exposed to two oxidizing species simultaneously. By modelling diffusion, interfacial reactions, and oxide-layer growth, it shows how the two oxidants may compete or interact to influence oxidation rate, phase formation, and oxide stability.
