Phase-field modeling of corrosion kinetics under dual-oxidants

(Literature Review)

Detail

This project aims to review the literature on phase-field modeling of corrosion kinetics under dual-oxidants. The review will focus on the theoretical framework, numerical methods, and applications of phase-field models in simulating corrosion processes in the presence of multiple oxidizing species. The goal is to provide a comprehensive understanding of the current state of research in this area and identify potential directions for future studies.

This review uses MATLAB as a tool to implement and visualize the phase-field models discussed in the literature. The MATLAB code will be used to simulate corrosion kinetics under dual-oxidants, allowing for a better understanding of the underlying mechanisms and the effects of different parameters on the corrosion process.
